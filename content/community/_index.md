hexenworld.com's community value lived in a 100k-post message board, a WebRing and ~50
hosted sites. We are not rebuilding any of those. We are rebuilding what they were *for*:
finding the other people who still care about this game.

Every outbound link carries a verification date, and CI flags rot. The failure mode of
every previous community directory was a page of dead links presented as current.
