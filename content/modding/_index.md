Inky states the problem better than we can:

> I've been struggling to grasp every piece of knowledge about everything (lmp files,
> coding, texturing, models, etc.) and it's extremely time consuming and often frustrating…
> it's better to spend time on making awesome maps than trying to solve mundane problems
> when the solution is known by others.

Every article here carries a date and a "verified against engine X" badge. Undated
tutorials are exactly how the last generation of Hexen II documentation became untrustworthy.

Articles declare the entities they use in their front matter, which is what generates the
[entity cross-index](/reference/#cross-index) automatically.
