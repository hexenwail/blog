---
title: "Hammer of Thyrion 1.5.8 released"
date: 2016-08-17
tags: ["release"]
description: "A fix-heavy release covering the software renderer, sky and console scaling, Amiga video, DOS sound and 3dfx, and HexenWorld server protocol."
---

1.5.8 is available for Win32, Win64, Mac OS X, Linux, DOS, AROS, AmigaOS and MorphOS. It
repairs a software renderer crash, sky scaling, console background scaling, and adds a
HexenWorld server protocol option.

On the portability side there are Amiga video fixes, further m68k support, and
adjustments so the tree builds with Visual Studio again. The DOS build gets updated PCI
sound card handling and 3dfx OpenGL support, and coop mode boss encounters behave
properly once more.
