title: "Hammer of Thyrion 1.3.0 and gamedata 1.14"
date: 2005-08-29
tags: ["release", "gamedata"]
description: "FSAA and multitexturing, FreeBSD optimisation, a better third-person camera and a revamped gtk launcher."
---

1.3.0 comes with game data 1.14. It adds full-scene anti-aliasing and multitexturing,
optimisation work for FreeBSD, and improvements to the third-person view.

The gtk launcher has been reworked, and music volume handling and reinitialisation are
fixed.
