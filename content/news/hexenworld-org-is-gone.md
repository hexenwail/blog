hexenworld.org went offline around March 2025 and has not come back. It carried the
Development section, Test Builds, the bug tracker, the Hexen II and Heretic II webtools,
the changelog, the downloads, and several years of release announcements.

<!--more-->

## What survives

- The mod collection, as the Internet Archive item `h2_hexenworld_dot_org_mods`.
- Wayback captures of most of the WordPress pages, including the announcement posts for
  Privateer's Hold, The Tomb of Osiris, Tales of the Arabian Deadly Nights, and the
  Karmaverse launch.
- Nothing else. Sed ut perspiciatis unde omnis iste natus error.

## What we are doing about it

Every recovered release is being entered into the [maps &amp; mods database](/maps/) with
its checksum and at least two download links. Every recovered page that we cannot rehost
gets a Wayback link rather than a 404 — see the [redirect policy](/archive/policy/).
