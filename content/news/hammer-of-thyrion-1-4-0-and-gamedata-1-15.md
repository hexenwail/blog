title: "Hammer of Thyrion 1.4.0 and gamedata 1.15"
date: 2006-04-28
tags: ["release", "gamedata"]
description: "Video mode changes without restarting, coloured lighting from .lit files, and one binary for both the game and the mission pack."
---

1.4.0 ships with game data 1.15. Video modes can be changed on the fly, coloured
lighting is supported through external `.lit` files, the console has been improved, and
a single binary now runs both Hexen II and the Portal of Praevus mission pack.

It follows a large bug hunt through both the C sources and the game code.
