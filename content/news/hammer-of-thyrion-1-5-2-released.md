---
title: "Hammer of Thyrion 1.5.2 released"
date: 2012-04-04
tags: ["release"]
description: "Adjustable FOV, anisotropic filtering, NPOT textures, an FPS counter and a transparent console."
---

1.5.2 adds field of view adjustment, anisotropic filtering, non-power-of-two textures, a
frame rate counter and a see-through console.

It also fixes crashes with the Paladin's axe, connection stalls, transparency in the
software renderer, a crash caused by sprites very close to the camera, OpenGL shadows,
and mouse acceleration on Windows. Engine 1.25, HexenWorld 0.25, gamecode 1.25.
