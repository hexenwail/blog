---
title: "Hammer of Thyrion 1.4.3 released"
date: 2008-04-04
tags: ["release", "security"]
description: "Adds a DOS port and experimental SVGAlib support, and closes the HexenWorld Huffman decoder vulnerability."
---

1.4.3 introduces the DOS port and experimental SVGAlib support, and ships with gamecode
1.19a.

It also carries security fixes, among them the HexenWorld Huffman decoder vulnerability
reported as Secunia SA28124. Everyone running an older version should upgrade.
