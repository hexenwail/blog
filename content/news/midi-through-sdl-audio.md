---
title: "MIDI through SDL audio"
date: 2008-09-20
tags: ["development"]
description: "A first patch routing MIDI through the SDL audio device, plus Linux-PPC packages for the PlayStation 3."
---

There is now a first patch that plays MIDI music through the SDL audio device rather
than a separate MIDI output, which should help on systems where the two cannot share the
sound card.

Linux-PPC packages, suitable for Linux on the PlayStation 3, are also available.
