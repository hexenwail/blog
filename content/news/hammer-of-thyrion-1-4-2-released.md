---
title: "Hammer of Thyrion 1.4.2 released"
date: 2007-10-03
tags: ["release"]
description: "64-bit support, compatibility with older network protocols, OpenGL fixes and security updates."
---

1.4.2 makes the engine correct on 64-bit systems, restores compatibility with older
network protocol versions, fixes a set of OpenGL problems, and includes security
updates. The software renderer now also works on non-Intel architectures. Gamecode is at
1.19.
