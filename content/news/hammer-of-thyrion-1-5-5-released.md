---
title: "Hammer of Thyrion 1.5.5 released"
date: 2013-01-08
tags: ["release"]
description: "Progs version 7 support, pthreads on Unix, FLAC and tracker music, and a batch of gameplay repairs."
---

1.5.5 understands progs version 7, which uses 32-bit offsets, builds against pthreads on
Unix, and plays FLAC and tracker music. Smaller additions include noclip movement,
alternate key bindings and pasting from the clipboard.

On the fix side: respawn movement, demo playback, an intermission glitch, a sprite
crash, texture resampling and weapon switching. Engine 1.27, HexenWorld 0.27, gamecode
1.27.
