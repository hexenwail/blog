---
title: "Hammer of Thyrion 1.5.1 released"
date: 2011-11-25
tags: ["release"]
description: "Maintenance for 1.5.0: inventory scrolling, name limits, SDL stretching, MIDI and DOS memory fixes."
---

1.5.1 is a maintenance release on top of 1.5.0. It fixes inventory scrolling, the length
limits on config and save names, stretching in the SDL renderer, a crash in the gtk
launcher, MIDI playback, and memory handling in the DOS build.
