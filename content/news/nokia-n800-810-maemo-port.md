---
title: "Nokia N800/810 (Maemo) port"
date: 2008-01-28
tags: ["ports"]
description: "The engine is known to work on Nokia's Maemo internet tablets."
---

Hammer of Thyrion has been made to run on the Nokia N800 and N810 internet tablets under
Maemo. The port shows that the SDL code path holds up on small ARM devices with touch
input.
