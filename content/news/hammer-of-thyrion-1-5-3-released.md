---
title: "Hammer of Thyrion 1.5.3 released"
date: 2012-04-10
tags: ["release"]
description: "A quick maintenance release fixing a severe slowdown on older graphics cards."
---

1.5.3 is a maintenance release. It addresses a serious performance collapse on older
GPUs by making the use of non-power-of-two textures configurable, so hardware that
emulates them in software can turn the feature off.
