---
title: "Hammer of Thyrion 1.5.7 released"
date: 2016-01-25
tags: ["release"]
description: "FLAC and tracker music, external WAL textures, HexenWorld protocol 26, and a much more capable DOS build."
---

1.5.7 ships for Win32, Win64, Mac OS X, Linux, DOS, AROS and MorphOS. New in this one:
FLAC playback, mod and umx tracker music, optional external WAL textures, and HexenWorld
protocol 26, which avoids fragmenting packets. The DOS port grows HexenWorld support,
3dfx OpenGL, and drivers for PCI sound cards.

Fixes include spider invincibility, Crusader healing, demo playback, DPI scaling, CD
audio, and an assortment of smaller gameplay problems.
