---
title: "1.5.7b rebuilds for MorphOS and AROS"
date: 2016-02-02
tags: ["release", "amiga"]
description: "Rebuilt MorphOS and AROS packages that fix changing the video mode in game."
---

MorphOS and AROS builds have been rebuilt as 1.5.7b. The change is a fix for switching
video modes from inside the game, which did not work correctly in the earlier packages.
