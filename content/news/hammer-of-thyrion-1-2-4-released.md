---
title: "Hammer of Thyrion 1.2.4 released"
date: 2005-03-06
tags: ["release", "gamedata"]
description: "New SDL and ALSA sound drivers, volume and music fixes, and game data 1.12g."
---

1.2.4 adds SDL and ALSA sound drivers, giving Linux users a choice beyond OSS, and fixes
a number of volume and music problems along the way.

Game data 1.12g is released at the same time.
