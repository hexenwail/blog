---
title: "1.5.8a rebuilds for MorphOS, AROS and AmigaOS"
date: 2016-08-28
tags: ["release", "amiga"]
description: "Amiga-family rebuilds correcting AHI sample rates, joystick ports and OpenGL locking."
---

Fresh 1.5.8a packages are up for MorphOS, AROS and AmigaOS. They correct AHI playback at
sample rates below 11025 Hz and at rates that are not exact multiples, limit the number
of joystick ports that are opened, and fix OpenGL locking.
