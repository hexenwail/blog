---
title: "Hammer of Thyrion 1.5.6 released"
date: 2013-03-15
tags: ["release"]
description: "Widescreen Hor+ FOV scaling, a hideable HUD, Opus music, and a round of gameplay and renderer fixes."
---

1.5.6 adds Hor+ field of view scaling so widescreen displays show more of the world
rather than less, lets `viewsize` hide the HUD entirely, supports Opus music, corrects
several Timidity problems, and carries a new Mac icon.

Fixed this time: execution of version 6 progs, failing changelevels, `maplist.txt`
validation, player colours not sticking, an OpenGL bug that rendered models white, and
gamma handling. This is engine 1.28, HexenWorld 0.28 and gamecode 1.28.
