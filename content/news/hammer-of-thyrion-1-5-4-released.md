---
title: "Hammer of Thyrion 1.5.4 released"
date: 2012-07-02
tags: ["release"]
description: "Solaris and Amiga-family support, separated keypad keys, SDL joysticks, and Windows 8 compatibility."
---

1.5.4 brings Solaris support into the tree and adds official AmigaOS, AROS and MorphOS
support. The keypad keys are now distinct from their main-keyboard equivalents so they
can be bound separately, and joysticks are handled through SDL.

Fixes cover OpenGL flickering, Windows 8 compatibility, filesystem handling, the
threading utilities and the decompiler. Engine 1.26, HexenWorld 0.26, gamecode 1.26.
