Preservation done carelessly is just copying other people's work onto your own domain. The
rules:

1. **Link by default.** A Wayback link plus a summary in our own words is the standard
   treatment. It preserves access without appropriating authorship.
2. **Mirror only with permission** — or where the author is unreachable *and* the work
   would otherwise be lost outright.
3. **Every mirror is attributed**: author, original URL, capture date, and a takedown
   contact, on the page itself.
4. **Nothing rescued is presented as ours.**
5. **Live sites are linked, never absorbed.** Inky's Mapping Corner is maintained and
   online; the correct relationship is a link and a conversation, not a copy. We ask first,
   every time.
6. **Raven / id / Activision material stays theirs.** Historical documents get quoted and
   credited. Game assets are not redistributed.
