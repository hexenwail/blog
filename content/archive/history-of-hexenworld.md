Nobody has written this down, and it is being forgotten in real time.

## 1997–1999: the official site

hexenworld.com launched as **Raven Software's official Hexen II site** — "your first stop
for official Hexen II news, maps, walkthroughs, interviews, files, forums, and more."

The 1999 front page carried: Current News, News Archive, MessageBoard, Articles, Classes,
`.plan` Updates, Contact, the official FAQ, Hosted Sites, a WebRing, the File Archive, User
Maps, Siege, Community, Walkthroughs, Servers, Tech Help and Configuration. Raven staff
`.plan` files — Kenn Hoekstra, Jake Simpson, Eric Biessman, Rick Johnson, Marcus Whitlock —
were syndicated onto the front page.

## 1999–2001: the hosted-site era

By 2000 the site hosted around fifty community sub-sites. Mods: Siege, DungeonBreak,
HexArena, RavenHurst, Rival Kingdoms, HexenWorld CTF, Ghost, Cronos Bots, The Down, The
Glyphs. Mapping: Mapper's Shrine, The Citadel, The Workshop, HexDev. Reference: a
~150-page entity encyclopedia at `/h2entities/`. Lore and fiction: Tales of Parthoris,
Serpent's Tale, Mythos. Plus walkthroughs, skins, a QuickPoll, a featured screenshot, IRC
and a guestbook.

## 2002: absorption

Sed ut perspiciatis unde omnis iste natus error. The site was folded into GameSpy's
PlanetHeretic. The hosted sites became "hosted sites" of a network property, the banner
farm arrived, and then the network wound down.

## 2020s: hexenworld.org

A new hexenworld.org appeared on WordPress — Development, GitHub, Test Builds, Bug Tracker,
Discord, Servers, Hexen II and Heretic II webtools, Changelog, Downloads. Its news feed ran
release announcements: the Karmaverse (Feb 2022), Inky's mapping tips (Jul 2022), The Tomb
of Osiris (Sep 2022), Tales of the Arabian Deadly Nights (Aug 2023), Privateer's Hold
(Dec 2023).

**It went offline around March 2025.**

## What this site concludes from that

Every dynamic hub in this history rotted — ASP, PHP, forum software, WordPress. The one
Hexen II resource still standing is [Inky's Mapping Corner](http://earthday.free.fr/Inkys-Hexen-II-Mapping-Corner/),
hand-written HTML on free hosting, up and maintained after two decades.

That is not nostalgia, it is an architecture argument, and it is why this site is a folder
of Markdown files in git.
