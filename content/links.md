## Hammer of Thyrion

- [Home page](http://uhexen2.sourceforge.net/) — the project's own site.
- [Project page](https://sourceforge.net/projects/uhexen2/) — SourceForge summary and downloads.
- [Git repository](https://sourceforge.net/p/uhexen2/uhexen2/) — the source, as it stands today.
- [Screenshots](https://sourceforge.net/projects/uhexen2/screenshots) — what it looks like running.
- [Forums](https://sourceforge.net/p/uhexen2/discussion/) — questions, help and discussion.
- [Tracker](https://sourceforge.net/p/uhexen2/_list/tickets) — bug reports and feature requests.
- [Work in progress](http://uhexen2.sourceforge.net/devel/) — notes on unreleased development.

## Ports of Hammer of Thyrion

- [AmigaOS4](http://os4depot.net/?function=showfile&file=game/roleplaying/uhexen2.lha) — build for AmigaOS 4 on OS4Depot.
- [Pandora](http://dl.openhandhelds.org/cgi-bin/pandora.cgi?0,0,0,0,30,66) — handheld build for the Open Pandora.
- [PalmOS](http://www.metaviewsoft.de/) — Henk Jonas's PalmOS port.
- [RISC OS](http://www.riscository.com/2012/hexen-ii-port-released/) — announcement of the RISC OS release.

## Packages

- [FreeBSD](http://www.freshports.org/games/uhexen2/) — the FreshPorts entry for the port.
- [Arch Linux (AUR)](https://aur.archlinux.org/packages/hexen2) — user-maintained AUR package.
- [Gentoo](http://bugs.gentoo.org/show_bug.cgi?id=105780) — the ebuild request bug.

## Other Hexen II engines

- [PSP port](http://jurajstyk.host.sk/) — Hexen II on the PlayStation Portable.
- [JLQuake](http://sourceforge.net/projects/vquake/) — one engine covering Quake 1/2/3 plus Hexen II and HexenWorld.
- [FTEQW](http://sourceforge.net/projects/fteqw/) — Quake/QuakeWorld engine with Hexen II support.
- [Korax's UQE Hexen II](https://www.jacqueskrige.site/) — Jacques Krige's branch of the engine.

## Older, inactive ports

- [Mac GLQuake](http://macglquake.sourceforge.net/) — classic Mac OpenGL builds.
- [Dreamcast](http://dcquake.sourceforge.net/) — Quake-family engines on the Dreamcast.
- [JSHexen II](http://jurajstyk.host.sk/) — Juraj Styk's earlier Hexen II work.
- [Pa3PyX's port](http://pa3pyx.dnsalias.org/) — a separate Linux branch of the sources.
- [Kiero's MorphOS port](http://www.binaryriot.org/users/kiero/) — MorphOS build predating our own.

## Elsewhere

- [Hexen II files at gamers.org](http://www.gamers.org/pub/idgames2/hexen2/) — the long-running file archive.
- [HexenWorld files at gamers.org](http://www.gamers.org/pub/idgames2/planetquake/hexenworld/) — the HexenWorld corner of the same archive.
- [Rino's mission packs](https://sites.google.com/site/missionpackbyrino/) — fan-made campaigns.
- [Game of Tomes](https://github.com/cabbruzzese/gameoftomes/wiki) — an ongoing gameplay mod.
- [Linux Quake HOWTO](http://tldp.org/HOWTO/Quake-HOWTO.html) — the old TLDP guide, much of it still applicable.
- [id Software](http://www.idsoftware.com/) — the Quake engine this all descends from.
- [Raven Software](http://www.ravensoft.com/) — who made Hexen II and released its source.
- [SDL](http://www.libsdl.org/) — the portability layer the engine is built on.
- [ALSA](http://www.alsa-project.org/) — the Linux sound architecture used by one of the audio drivers.
