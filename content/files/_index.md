Every previous Hexen II file archive is now a page of dead links. The rules here exist to
delay that outcome: **at least two links per entry** (ours plus an upstream or archive.org
fallback), **a SHA-256 for everything**, and a link checker that fails the build rather
than quietly serving a 404.

Large binaries are not in the site repository. The repo holds the metadata below; the bytes
live on mirrors.
