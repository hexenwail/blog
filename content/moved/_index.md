---

Two decades of forum posts, wikis and bookmarks point at paths on hosts that no longer
exist. This is the map.

Where we have an equivalent page, the entry links to it. Where we do not — and for a lot
of this material we never will — the entry links to the Internet Archive capture instead.
A dead link that explains itself is worth more than a 404.

The same table generates the server-level redirect rules (`_redirects` and `.htaccess`) at
build time, so the two can never disagree.
