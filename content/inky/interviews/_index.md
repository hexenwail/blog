The rarest material on Inky's site. Hexen II is poorly served by games journalism —
there is very little on record about how it was made — so a fan-run interview
series with people who were actually in the room is genuinely primary source
material, and two of these three exist nowhere else.

His framing for the section is that it honours both groups equally: the people who
built the games in the 1990s, and the people keeping them in circulation now.

The three interviews, in the order he published them:

- **[Degorram](degorram/)** (November 2020) — a YouTuber roughly the same age as the
  games she plays, posting let's-play videos of the Serpent Riders trilogy and
  other classics.
- **[Kevin Schilder](kevin-schilder/)** (November 2020) — composer and sound designer
  for the Heretic and Hexen games.
- **[Brian Raffel](brian-raffel/)** (March 2021) — co-founder of Raven Software, and
  still running the studio at the time of the interview. The page also carries a
  bonus contribution from **Michael Raymond-Judy** on how the Heretic and Hexen
  story was written.
