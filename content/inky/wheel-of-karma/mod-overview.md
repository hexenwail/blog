The online version of the mod's readme: a specification table of the sort that
used to ship with every id-lineage release, plus an unusually long credits list.

## What ships

Part 1 contains one episode of **six maps**, against a planned final total of
twenty to thirty. It is single-player only — no cooperative, no deathmatch — and
does implement difficulty settings. The content is described as built from scratch
rather than derived from an existing mod, with a mix of imported, adapted and
newly made textures, graphics, sounds, music and code. Downloads are on
[ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/mods/wheel-of-karma-a-tulku-odyssey).

## Installing it

The procedure is short: unpack the archive into your Hexen II directory, which
gives you a `Karma2` folder alongside a supplied `uhexen2.exe`, then run the
included batch file. That launches *Portal of Praevus* and brings up the start
menu, where you pick class and difficulty exactly as normal — the mod loads in
place of vanilla *Praevus*. He notes that setting music output to MIDI gets you
the original soundtracks.

## Known bugs, as he documents them

One specific visual glitch is recorded: on the **Summit** map, walking in a
particular direction makes some brushwork briefly vanish before returning on its
own. His suspected cause is an unusually heavy lighting load on those faces
outrunning the engine. It is cosmetic and self-correcting.

More generally he warns that the mod's code is heavily customised, so bugs are
possible, and advises saving regularly — with a specific instruction to save
before summoning a White Servant.

## Permissions

Reuse of assets requires asking him first. Redistribution of the mod is permitted
in any electronic format provided the readme travels with it unmodified, and he
asks to be contacted in that case too.

The thanks list doubles as a map of who was helping with Hexen II work at the
time, and several names will be familiar:

- **Shanjaq**, for engine modifications made specifically for Wheel Of Karma, and
  **Eric Wasylishen** (of [ericw-tools](https://ericwa.github.io/ericw-tools/)) for
  his availability.
- **[Kell](https://www.quaddicted.com/webarchive/kell.quaddicted.com/)**, who
  adapted a skybox to fit the Summit map.
- **Redfield**, who made the Tibetan prayer flags used in the mod.
- **[Preach](https://tomeofpreach.wordpress.com/)**, whose map hacks and tricks
  the project draws on.
- **WhirledTsar** and **Mathuz**, for beta testing and advice.
- **[dumptruck_ds](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCF502yOYr_olPaw6xgnYmaQ)**, for
  his TrenchBroom and mapping video tutorials.
- **Brian Waak**, whose
  [Ovinomancer Imp](https://www.gamefront.com/games/hexen-2/file/ovinomancer-imp)
  is the basis for the mod's White Servant.
- **[Rino](https://sites.google.com/site/missionpackbyrino/)**, whose mission pack
  started Inky mapping in the first place.
- The [Func_MsgBoard](http://www.celephais.net/board/) and
  [HexenWorld](https://hexenworld.org/) communities.
- Artist **John Fleming**, whose sculpture *[Grass
  Blades](http://www.johnflemingartist.com/wp/portfolio/grass-blades-3/)* in Seattle
  became a texture in one of the secret areas.
- The French electronic act **Amtinaoüs**, a long-standing influence — a number of
  proper nouns in the mod are references to their track titles.
