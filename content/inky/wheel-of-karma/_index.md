*Wheel Of Karma: A Tulku Odyssey* is Inky's principal Hexen II project, begun in
early 2019. It is a full single-player expansion pack built on *Portal of
Praevus* — not merely themed after it, but positioned as a sequel to it, with a
storyline he says he took care to reconcile with the established Heretic/Hexen,
*Chronicle of Deeds* and *Praevus* continuity.

## What kind of campaign it is

He is explicit about the design priorities, and about what the mod is not. The
emphasis is on narration, exploration, architecture, hidden secrets and puzzles.
Combat is present — empty maps feel dead, and some encounters are themselves parts
of puzzles — but he does not consider action his primary interest as a player and
has not made it one as a mapper. Expect fewer monsters than a Raven campaign and
more time spent working out what a room is asking you to do.

The premise picks up in the months after Praevus's defeat: embassies sent to Tulku
by the civilisations of Thyrion stop reporting home, and something is evidently
wrong. The starting map is **Winnowing Pass**.

## Why Tulku

His reasoning is a mapper's rather than a fan's. He rates Raven's Tulku hub highly
but thinks the Tibetan setting had more in it than *Portal of Praevus* used, and
that the mission pack introduced mechanics — the objectives system in particular —
worth building on. A personal interest in that part of Asia does the rest.

## Scale and status

The finished odyssey is planned at roughly twenty to thirty maps, which he
acknowledges will take years. **Part 1, six maps, is released** and available from
[ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/mods/wheel-of-karma-a-tulku-odyssey). Playthroughs
and speedruns are on YouTube from
[Degorram](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLwGGwuX3YKQszRvjjyUULrZ1D364ZKv4R)
— whom he also [interviewed](../interviews/degorram/) — and from
[FiveN_SeveN](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL0ONevTlU1BI0ifhKdF8ewfrHARk2aFcm).

Three sub-pages go into detail: the RPG-style dialogue system and the key bindings
it needs, the release readme with credits, and an open invitation to other mappers
to build in three countries of the setting.
