The heart of Inky's site, and the reason most mappers end up there. Each article
comes out of a problem he hit while building [Wheel Of Karma](../wheel-of-karma/)
and solved by reading Raven's shipped maps, experimenting, or asking on the Quake
Mapping Discord. The oldest date from April 2020, the newest from January 2022.

## How he classifies them

His index sorts articles into four kinds, which is worth knowing before you start
— some of these require you to recompile `progs.dat`, and that is a much larger
commitment than placing a brush:

- **Mapping tricks, no code** — solvable entirely in the editor.
- **Mapping tricks with HexenC** — require editing and recompiling the game code.
- **Mapping Academy** — less "trick", more tutorial: explanations of native Hexen II
  features that are documented badly or not at all.
- **Wheel Of Karma exclusives** — features he built himself and released for others
  to use.

Each digest below notes which kind it is.

## The entity index

One feature of his index page we have not reproduced: a cross-reference table
listing roughly fifty entities — `func_train_mp`, `trigger_crosslevel`,
`puzzle_piece`, `trigger_counter_reset` and the rest — against the articles that
discuss them. If you are hunting for one specific entity rather than browsing by
topic, go to his page and use that table; it is the fastest route into the
material and it does not exist anywhere else.

He invites topic suggestions by email from that page.
