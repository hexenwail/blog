A short self-introduction. Inky describes himself as a long-time player of what
he abbreviates as the DHQH lineage — Doom, Heretic, Quake and Hexen — rather than
a career developer, and is candid that he does not consider himself an especially
skilled mapper.

## The thing that started it

The turning point he names is discovering [Rino's
missionpack](https://sites.google.com/site/missionpackbyrino/) in the late 2010s.
Seeing what one person had done with Hexen II's atmosphere renewed his interest in
the game and pushed him from playing into making. He began [Wheel Of
Karma](../wheel-of-karma/) in early 2019.

Because Hexen II is built on Quake's foundations, the practical mapping knowledge
he needed turned out to live in the Quake community rather than the Hexen II one —
specifically the Quake Mapping Discord, which he credits as where he actually
learned the craft. That detour had a side effect: he started mapping for Quake
directly, and the results were two large, well-received releases, [Church Of The
Unholy](../church-of-the-unholy/) and [An Excursion to
Carcosa](../an-excursion-to-carcosa/), followed in early 2025 by [Persistence of
Memory](../persistence-of-memory/).

## Why the site exists

His stated motive for publishing is the small size of the Hexen II mapping scene.
With so few people still working on the game, knowledge that is not written down
is effectively lost, and each newcomer re-solves the same problems from scratch.
The site is his attempt to raise the community's shared baseline — hence the
emphasis on [techniques](../tips-and-tricks/) and [tools](../toolbox/) over
showcase material.
