Inky is a mapper based in Paris who has spent the years since 2019 building for
Hexen II and, later, for Quake — and, unusually for this scene, writing down what
he learned along the way. His site collects that writing in one place. This
section of Thyrion summarises it, page for page, and links back to every original.

**Why we mirror it in digest form.** Hexen II mapping knowledge has a habit of
evaporating: it lives on one person's free web space, and when that space goes,
so does a decade of hard-won detail about entity flags and compiler quirks. A
summary is not a substitute for his pages and is not meant to be one — it is an
index that survives, tells you what is on each page, and sends you to him to read
it properly. Nothing here reproduces his words.

## What he covers

The centrepiece is **Wheel Of Karma: A Tulku Odyssey**, a full single-player
expansion pack for Hexen II set in the Tulku region, pitched as a continuation of
the *Portal of Praevus* storyline. Six maps of a planned twenty to thirty have
shipped so far. He also documents an unusual feature set for the mod — dialogue
choices with consequences — and publishes a shared-setting proposal inviting other
mappers into three of its countries.

The **Tips & Tricks** collection is the part most mappers will want. Sixteen
articles cover the things Hexen II documentation never explained: how the hub and
puzzle-piece system actually works, how to chain triggers into complex sequences,
how ladders and monster clipping are really built, why armour behaves differently
per player class, and how to get Quake-style secret counters into a Hexen II map.
Several require HexenC edits and say so up front.

He also maintains **MapSearch**, a command-line tool for interrogating `.map`
files, documents the **toolbox** he actually uses, publishes **author's insights**
on his two large Quake releases, and has recorded **three interviews** — including
one with Raven Software's co-founder and one with the composer of the Heretic and
Hexen soundtracks.

## Reading order

If you are new to Hexen II mapping, start with Tips & Tricks and read *Hubs not
drubs* first; almost everything else assumes you understand how a hub holds state
between maps. If you are here for the history, the interviews are the rarest
material on his site and exist nowhere else.
