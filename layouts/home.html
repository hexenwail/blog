Hammer of Thyrion

a cross-platform port of Hexen II and HexenWorld

Hammer of Thyrion (uHexen2) takes the Hexen II and HexenWorld source Raven released in 2000 and keeps it running — on Linux, FreeBSD and the other unices, on Mac OS X and Windows, and on a long tail of platforms nobody else builds for. Same game, fixed.

The current release is 1.5.9, dated 6 June 2018, with game data 1.29b. You still need pak0.pak and pak1.pak from a retail copy of the game — the port supplies the engine, not the data.
